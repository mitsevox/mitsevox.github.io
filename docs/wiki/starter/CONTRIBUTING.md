## Authority

Only OWNER_LOGIN authorizes and performs main merges. SHIP, passing CI and owner clearance
are distinct states. Agents do not alter branch protection or dependency access to fix a patch.

## Work

Claim bounded scope and dependencies; use an isolated branch/worktree from current main.
Establish target behavior, types and boundaries with reproducible evidence before reconstruction.
Document inferred names separately from recovered original names. Keep unsupported regions opaque.
Compile and compare using approved unit/library profiles. Keep useful partial source in place,
with original bytes retained in the verified linked build until promotion is validated.

## Integrity

No unsupported per-function flags, patched output, inline instruction substitutions, invented
padding/control flow, excluded mismatches or boundary manipulation. Record compiler reasoning
and uncertainties in metadata, not matching commentary in reconstructed source.

## Review and final validation

Self-check, then independent blind accuracy and fresh hostile review. Source mismatch alone is
not a review failure. Evidence, source fidelity and comparison integrity still must pass.
After fixes/main refresh, review the delta and affected dependencies; carry forward adequate
coverage with its revision. Required CI covers the final integrated inputs.
Review records identify full revision, reviewer, scope, evidence, findings and resolution.
